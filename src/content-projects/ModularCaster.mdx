## Project Overview

The objective of this university group project was to develop a safe and efficient method for maneuvering and securing medical equipment within the confined, high-stakes environment of an air ambulance. The development process was heavily grounded in upfront market identification, stakeholder interviews, and rigorous research into aerospace and medical industry standards.

## Mechanical Design and Features

The final output is a fully functional mechanical design tailored for aviation requirements.
* **Modular Architecture:** The system utilizes a modular design, enabling it to be easily adapted or reconfigured for various types of medical equipment and mounting constraints.
* **Locking Mechanism:** A core engineering focus was the development of a complex locking linkage. This linkage incorporates a physical safety lockout designed to securely anchor the caster during flight, preventing unintended movement during turbulence or high-G maneuvers.

## Compliance and Safety

Given the strict regulatory environment of aerospace engineering, the caster system was designed to be fully compliant with relevant aviation and medical standards. The mechanical integrity of the design was verified through extensive safety calculations and computational simulations to ensure it could withstand required operational loads and stress factors.

## Manufacturing and Results

Alongside the mechanical design and simulations, a full manufacturing plan was developed to outline how the system could be fabricated and assembled at scale. The design was subsequently prototyped and presented to a panel, where it achieved the highest possible grade for the course.
